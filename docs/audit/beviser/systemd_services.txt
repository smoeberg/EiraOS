# Systemd Services - Beviser

## Systemd Service Files

### 1. eira.target
**Fil:** `systemd/eira.target`

```ini
[Unit]
Description=EiraOS core IPC daemon target
Documentation=https://github.com/smoeberg/EiraOS
Wants=eira-stated.service eira-identityd.service eira-fleetd.service
Wants=eira-veritasd.service eira-intentd.service eira-graphd.service
After=eira-stated.service eira-identityd.service eira-fleetd.service
After=eira-veritasd.service eira-intentd.service eira-graphd.service

[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target
```

**Analyse:**
- ✅ Alle 6 EIRA daemons inkluderet
- ✅ Korrekte dependencies (After=)
- ✅ Installeres som del af multi-user.target

---

### 2. eira-stated.service
**Fil:** `systemd/eira-stated.service`

```ini
[Unit]
Description=EiraOS State IPC daemon
Documentation=https://github.com/smoeberg/EiraOS
PartOf=eira.target
Before=eira.target

[Service]
Type=simple
User=eira
Group=eira
WorkingDirectory=/usr/lib/eira-os
Environment=PYTHONUNBUFFERED=1
Environment=PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1
Environment=PYTHONPATH=/usr/lib/eira-os
EnvironmentFile=-/etc/eira/eira.conf
ExecStart=/usr/bin/eira-stated
Restart=always
RestartSec=2s
RuntimeDirectory=eira
RuntimeDirectoryMode=0770
RuntimeDirectoryPreserve=yes
StateDirectory=eira
StateDirectoryMode=0770
LogsDirectory=eira
LogsDirectoryMode=0770
UMask=0117
ProtectSystem=strict
ProtectHome=true
PrivateTmp=true
PrivateDevices=true
NoNewPrivileges=true
CapabilityBoundingSet=
AmbientCapabilities=
ProtectKernelTunables=true
ProtectKernelModules=true
ProtectControlGroups=true
LockPersonality=true
RestrictAddressFamilies=AF_UNIX
RestrictSUIDSGID=true
ReadWritePaths=/data /run/eira /var/log/eira
AppArmorProfile=/usr/bin/eira-stated

[Install]
WantedBy=eira.target
```

**Analyse:**
- ✅ Kører som `eira` user (ikke root)
- ✅ System-level service
- ✅ Security hardening (ProtectSystem, PrivateTmp, etc.)
- ✅ Network: AF_UNIX only (ingen inet/inet6)
- ✅ AppArmor profil specificeret
- ✅ Runtime directories med korrekte permissions

RestartSec=2s
RuntimeDirectory=eira
RuntimeDirectoryMode=0770
RuntimeDirectoryPreserve=yes
StateDirectory=eira
StateDirectoryMode=0770
LogsDirectory=eira
LogsDirectoryMode=0770
UMask=0117
ProtectSystem=strict
ProtectHome=true
PrivateTmp=true
PrivateDevices=true
NoNewPrivileges=true
CapabilityBoundingSet=
AmbientCapabilities=
ProtectKernelTunables=true
ProtectKernelModules=true
ProtectControlGroups=true
LockPersonality=true
RestrictAddressFamilies=AF_UNIX AF_INET AF_INET6

IPAddressAllow=localhost

- ✅ ReadWritePaths: /data/eira-fleet
